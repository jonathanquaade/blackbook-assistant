# Freelance branding shortlist, identity systems

Prepared 2026-06-15. Brief: a branding project needing identity system / brand
designers, ideally someone who can build a bold, system-driven identity with a
strong typographic backbone (the kind of work a challenger rebrand needs).

All of these are in the Notion DB as `Entry Status: New` (Source: "Found using
Claude"), so the scout can pick them up later. This doc pulls out only the ones
who look freelance and potentially available NOW. Everything below is scout
research, unverified: treat availability and detail as leads to confirm, not facts.

Reading order: Tier 1 = reach out first. Tier 2 = available but with a real
caveat. Tier 3 = support or qualify-first, not an identity-systems lead.

---

## Tier 1, confirmed freelance + identity/brand core (reach out first)

**Leonhard Laupichler** — Berlin, Germany. Highest priority.
- Site: leonhardlaupichler.com
- Identity, type design, creative direction.
- Independent since June 2023 (his own words). Nearly four years at Bureau Borsche
  (Munich): Supreme collections FW20 to SS22 with Mirko Borsche and Jean-Pierre
  Meier, plus the Athens Kallithea FC identity. Six months at Bus.Group (Berlin)
  before that. Co-creator of the New Aesthetic type books.
- Why he fits: football-club and fashion identity work shows he can build bold,
  system-driven brands on a strong typographic backbone. Most directly on-brief.
- Availability: confirmed freelance; confirm current capacity.

**Daan Rietbergen** — Utrecht, Netherlands.
- Site: daanrietbergen.com. LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/drietbergen
- Visual identity, poster design, typography.
- Studio Dumbar 2014 to 2019 (Amsterdam Sinfonietta poster series, Dutch National
  Police) before going independent.
- Why he fits: Studio Dumbar pedigree means large-scale, flexible identity
  systems, the exact discipline a big rebrand needs.
- Availability: confirmed freelance; confirm current capacity.

**Charlie Nash (Nash Projects)** — London / Stockholm.
- Site: nash-projects.com. LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/charliewnash
- Brand identity, packaging, art direction, print. 10+ years.
- Ex North Kingdom and Amore (Stockholm). Work includes the Founderful (Swiss VC)
  identity and Absolut campaign work.
- Why he fits: identity + packaging + art-direction range, and a Nordic/UK base.
- Availability: strongest "available now" signal of the group, actively
  advertising it ("rolling into spring with availability").

**Sophia Brinkgerd** — Berlin, Germany.
- Art direction, graphic design, identity. Fashion/culture sensibility.
- Around three years at bus.group (Kreuzberg) and time at Apple before freelancing.
- Long-time collaborator with Leonhard Laupichler and Philipp Bulk, so she could
  pair with Laupichler as a known, tested team.
- Availability: confirmed freelance. (No portfolio URL captured yet, find before
  outreach.)
