MAWO

founded by a couple—Maria (model) and Sergiusz (lawyer), is a sustainable fashion label creating genderless and trend-defying designs from deadstock fabrics.

Inspired by Carl Sagan’s Pale Blue Dot, the brand identity reflects MAWO’s focus on Earth, symbolized by a blurred blue dot and consistent use of blue accents, such as the signature blue button on MAWO’s shirts.

The name “MAWO,” derived from the Polish word mało (meaning “little” or “few”), embodies the label’s commitment to minimal production, timeless design, and reduced environmental impact.

The concept I developed set the tone for the brand’s launch campaign, photographed by the duo Kulesza & Pik. Since its inception, MAWO has continued to evolve its vision, collaborating with various talents to keep the story alive and ever-evolving.
