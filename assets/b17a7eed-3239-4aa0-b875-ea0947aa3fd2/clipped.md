David Michon. Bio. Work. Conversations. Contact.

I am a consultant, editorial director and, in the broadest senses, a producer and editor. What I offer is offer good judgement, strategic ideas – plus the experience and insight into how to realise them – and a network of ready collaborators and contributors, from graphic designers to architects, photographers, artists, and writers.

I am the former editor of Icon magazine, and former managing editor of Monocle magazine and Winkreative creative agency. My current and past clients include: Acne Studios, G.F Smith, Ask Us For Ideas, the Neue Zürcher Zeitung, Swarovski and the Peabody Group. I have collaborated with design agencies such as Pentagram, dn&co., Made Thought and Studio Lowrie.

Originally from Winnipeg, I have lived in London since 2008.
