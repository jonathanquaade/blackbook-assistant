Identity and website design for Gitpod, an ephemeral dev environment product. This project was done with my team at MAD. We refreshed Gitpod's logo and branding, introducing a kumquat colour theme throughout and simplifying their website to make the extremely powerful product more approachable.

We worked with the incredible Vic to create fun 3D elements that bring life to the brand and help explain the benefits of Gitpod. My role included identity design, art direction, and web design.
