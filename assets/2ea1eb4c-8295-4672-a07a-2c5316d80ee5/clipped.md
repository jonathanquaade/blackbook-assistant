In August (2020) I was approached to create the official lyric music video for the new song of Llusion ft. Cautious Clay.

The title of the song is Sad4Good, the lyrics talk on subjects like isolation and being on your lowest point mentally whereas the sound itself is very upbeat and happy like. It was this juxtaposition which is also very present within my work that made it a perfect match.
