This website is Disabled

When a website is down, when access has been revoked, or when a feature is not working, we are told ‘This website is disabled.’

The internet seems to think disabled means revoked access, not working, down and broken. But, to us being Disabled means so much more.

This website is Disabled.

Just like us.

And we will be represented in society as we are, rather than how we are perceived to be.

#CriticalAxis is a community-driven project that collects and analyzes Disability representation in media and advertising.
