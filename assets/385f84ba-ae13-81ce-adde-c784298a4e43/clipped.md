Overview

Proof helps law firms manage service of process, where documents are delivered by real people and assignments change often. When work moved between servers, clients couldn't tell what had happened or what was current.

I owned the experience from early vision through final design execution, with special care put into designing the new serve tracker and chat widget.

Problems

Important information was split across multiple pages

When jobs got reassigned, the story reset and users were left in the dark.

30% of chats only existed because the product didn't make job status clear.

A timeline that tells the whole story

Clients see exactly where their serve stands, what's happened, and what comes next.

We tested close to a hundred scenarios: delays, reassignments, failed attempts, multiple addresses, and edge cases. The tracker handles all of them.

All your chats in one place

Every conversation from every job, past and present. No switching required.

Every attempt, documented and verifiable

Location, photos, and server notes for each visit. When questions come up, the answers are already there.

Multiple addresses, one clear view

Clients can add, approve, or reject service addresses in one place.

No more digging through emails for documents

Documents stay with the serve, even when jobs get reassigned or take unexpected turns.

A bird's eye view of every job

Firms can see all their serves at a glance, with clear insight into how each one is progressing.

What we solved

The redesigned experience reduced confusion, lowered repeat support requests, and rebuilt trust. Clients gained a clearer understanding of job status and next steps, while internal teams aligned around a shared source of truth that made the experience easier to support and operate.

Reflection

Coding with AI made it clear that if you can articulate what's in your head, you can design almost any interaction directly in code. Instead of translating ideas through layers of tooling, I was able to shape the product in real time and get it to look and feel exactly how I imagined it, down to the smallest interactions.

Performance, data modeling, security, and deployment also became things I had to consider for the first time end to end. Designing something entirely for myself removed the usual friction and allowed me to go deep into every shadow, hover state, micro-interaction and easing curve.
