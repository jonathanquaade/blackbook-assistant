Beta today.Better tomorrow.

By the latest estimates, the average user spends 413 minutes (just over 7 hours) online. Per. Day. Which means whether you’re a fast-scaling DTC ecommerce startup or an established global business, your brand needs to live in the internet.

Why? Because unless you’re selling walking frames to octogenarians, that’s where your audience lives too. (And even then... you know?) And because the Internet is a dynamic, dialogue-driven environment, you need a dynamic, dialogue-driven brand. One that’s designed digital from the ground up.

Drawing on nearly 200 years of science and philosophy, our Memetic Brand Framework™ creates brand platforms that are based on Darwin’s original principles. Our brands are designed not just be Chosen in a noisy environment, but then also Interpreted by diverse audience (from stakeholders to customers) and Shared within the culture without losing their meaning.

Our flexible brand frameworks are built on the logic of Atomic Design: a proven framework for for building efficient, scalable systems across teams with diverse skill sets. Platforms, not uniforms.

The internet is not a museum. It’s an ever-changing environment where ideas, norms and trends spread and develop with ever-increasing intensity. At any given moment in time, a brand is the dialogue between a company’s internal vision and their customer’s interpretation. So like a digital product, a digital brand needs to constantly iterate; to evolve and respond to market forces. Beta Today, Better Tomorrow.

We can wax philosophical about this stuff all day. Or at least for 27 minutes. If you want to know more about how it all fits together, and see some sweet gifs and peak 2022 references that may or may not age well, check out our Memetic Branding in the Digital Age talk from Figma’s Config2022.
