Alexis Mark is the shared pseudonym of Marie Grønkær, Kristoffer Li & Martin Bek, and an office for graphic design, creative direction and communication strategies located in Copenhagen.

With an emphasis on identity we help artists, institutions and companies communicate who they are by developing and visualising their core narratives.

Our work ranges from commissions in branding and identities, publications and campaign design, through self-organised autonomous projects, to design-education, exhibition-making and curating. We produce identities, publications, websites, exhibitions, art direction, campaigns, typefaces and more.

Alexis Mark is educated at Yale School of Art (USA) and The Gerrit Rietveld Academie (NL).

Additionally, Alexis Mark is the founder and organizer of the exhibition– and project space Annual Reportt.
