About —

Meg Jordan is a fashion and portrait photographer based in London. Her work aims to capture moments of stillness, fragility and freedom. Elevating untold stories and promoting self love, she enjoys photographing real moments of internal thought. Referencing theatre, french new wave cinema and early 90's youth culture, her work is a vibrant fusion of unique beauty and a narrative wider than the frame.

She has honed her craft for over a decade working in-house alongside Nick Knight, Miles Aldridge & Richard Foster.

‍

She has worked with Kanye West / ASOS / Ivy Park / New Balance / Yeezy / Dom Pérignon / Tom Ford / Fendi / Machine-A / British Vogue / The National Portrait Gallery / Uber / SHOWstudio / V Magazine

Her work has been published in The Royal Academy / Schon Magazine / TIME Magazine / The Barbican / L’Officiel Hommes / Stern / AnOther Magazine / Numero / Obvious Magazine
