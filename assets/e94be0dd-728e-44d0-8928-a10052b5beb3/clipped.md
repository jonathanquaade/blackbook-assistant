As a bespoke production house, we tailor our processes to suit the individual needs of each project.

We use the industry’s most innovative and sustainable materials to achieve the level of quality our clients expect.

We’ve become industry leaders because we deliver beautiful packaging on brand, on time, and on budget.

Our team is nimble and battle tested. We take a rigorous approach to providing the smoothest end-to-end service.

As a guide we use the following timeframes for our projects.

From the outset, our team is available to assist you in choosing the right materials, printing and finishes for your project. We can also provide custom die-lines and artwork to help you visualise designs.

In a few words – we are here for you. We are here to support you in achieving the very best results.

We quote on the specifics, the nitty gritty – materials, quantities, sizing and finishing . With all the information at hand, you can make informed decisions on the best fit for your project.

During this pre-production phase, we ensure the project is just as you envisaged. Both machine and digital samples are available and can be sent to you or directly to your client for approval.

From here, we can make tweaks and adjustments before moving into production.

Timing varies depending on the size and complexity of an order. The production period includes material sourcing, printing, die-cutting, hand-finishing, assembly and packaging for distribution.

4—25 days

As the last step in our service, our agents book the most cost-effective transport from our factory to any location around the world. Whether it’s direct to your door or several warehouses, whether you are sending 500 units or shipping container loads, we are there every step of the way.

We can also split orders across sea and air transport to ensure you get what you need, when you need it.

As a Forest Stewardship Council (FSC) certificate holder, we are dedicated to responsible production practices and providing quality sustainable materials to our clients.

We source the finest sustainable paper; we specify cardboard made from up to 80% recycled materials; and we work with printers who use soy- and water-based inks, an eco-friendly and effective alternative to petroleum-based ink.
